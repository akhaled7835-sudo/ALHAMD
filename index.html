About ALHAMD

A finishing and contracting partner for real projects

ALHAMD is a finishing and contracting company specializing in interior finishing works for residential and commercial projects.

We work with engineering offices, contractors, and project owners to deliver reliable finishing solutions with a focus on quality, coordination, and timely execution.

Our Services

Complete finishing solutions tailored to project requirements

Gypsum & Ceilings

Gypsum board, suspended ceilings and decorative ceiling works.

Painting Works

Interior and exterior painting and finishing works.

Flooring & Tiles

Ceramic, porcelain, marble and flooring installation works.

Electrical Works

Electrical installations and finishing works.

Plumbing Works

Plumbing installations and sanitary works.

Carpentry & Woodwork

Doors, kitchens, wardrobes and custom woodwork.

Complete Interior Finishing

Integrated finishing works for projects requiring multiple trades.
